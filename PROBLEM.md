Problem Definition: Fluento AI

## Problem Statement

English learners often struggle to convert grammar knowledge into accurate, spontaneous speech. Existing AI tools can correct individual mistakes but often fail to identify recurring weaknesses and adapt future practice.

## Problem

Learners repeatedly make the same grammatical mistakes during conversations. Current tools typically either over-correct and interrupt the conversation or under-correct and fail to address persistent mistakes. They rarely connect mistakes across multiple sessions to identify underlying weaknesses.

## Target Users

B1–B2 English learners seeking better speaking accuracy.
Self-taught learners without regular access to tutors.
Students and professionals preparing for academic or workplace communication.

## Current Workflow

Practice speaking with an AI chatbot, tutor, or language partner.
Receive individual corrections.
Manually identify recurring mistakes.
Search for lessons or exercises.
Practice the identified weakness separately.

## Existing Solutions

**AI Chatbots**: Good for conversation but limited long-term personalization.

**Language-learning Apps**: Structured learning but limited spontaneous conversation.

**Human Tutors**: Personalized and effective but costly and not always available.

## Limitations

Existing solutions create a gap between free conversation and personalized learning. They generally do not continuously track recurring mistakes and automatically turn them into targeted practice.

## Proposed Opportunity

Fluento AI is a voice-first Agentic English Tutor that:

* Allows natural spoken conversations.

* Analyzes speech for grammatical mistakes.

* Tracks mistakes across sessions.

* Identifies recurring weaknesses.

* Decides when intervention is necessary.

* Generates targeted micro-lessons or conversation activities.

* Adapts future conversations based on learner progress.

## Why AI?
AI is used where traditional rules are insufficient:

**Speech Recognition**: Converts natural speech into text.
**Language Analysis**: Understands context and identifies grammatical errors.
**Error Categorization**: Maps mistakes to underlying grammar concepts.
**Agentic Reasoning**: Decides what the learner should practice next based on historical performance.
**Content Generation**: Creates personalized explanations and exercises.

## Expected Impact

Fluento AI aims to provide continuous, personalized speaking practice while reducing recurring mistakes through targeted interventions and adaptive learning.
